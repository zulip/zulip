## More ways to try Zulip

- **Exploring options for your team?** [Create a free demo
  organization](/new/demo/) to see what Zulip could look like for you. No
  email required, and it only takes a minute.
- **Curious how others use Zulip?** View conversations in open organizations
  that have chosen to be listed in our [communities directory](/communities/).
- **Ready to get started?** [Create your organization](/new/) and invite
  your teammates.
