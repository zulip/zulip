Organized conversations, not tangled channels

  • In Slack, discussions are jumbled together in the channel, or tucked away in threads that are easy to miss.
  • Like email threads, Zulip topics create a separate space for each conversation, so discussions never get in each other's way, and a busy channel stays easy to follow.
  • See each message in context, and if a conversation drifts, move messages to a topic of their own or mark the topic ✓ resolved when done.
In fact now it seems strange to me to just fire off messages in Slack with no subject – that's chaos, madness. The genius of subject lines is that you can quickly and easily catch up on the messages you missed in your off-hours... This feature alone saves me hours a week.
— Zulip review in The Register

Built for asynchronous work

  • Slack is designed around being online: unread counts pile up while you're away, and the conversation moves on without you.
  • Zulip's inbox shows each conversation with unread messages. Read the ones you care about, skim or skip the rest, and reply in context even after hours or days away.
  • Busy leaders and experts can participate on their own time, without incessant @-mentions clamoring for their attention. Follow the topics where your timely input is needed.
Zulip's unique threading saves me well over an hour a day in working with our distributed team of engineers and PMs across 7+ time zones. We tried Slack, Mattermost, and other team chat products that claim to support threading, and nothing handles synchronous and asynchronous communication so intuitively.
— Jacinda Shelly, CTO, Doctor on Demand

Your chat history becomes a knowledge base

  • In Slack, finding an old discussion means sifting through unrelated chatter. On Slack's free plan, messages older than 90 days are hidden, and content older than a year is deleted.
  • With conversations organized by topic, you can review old discussions to understand past work, explanations, and decisions.
  • Topics make it easy to find the right conversation with Zulip's powerful full-text search, and you can link to a Zulip conversation from emails, docs, issue trackers, or anywhere else.
  • Zulip Cloud Free shows your 10,000 most recent messages. Older messages are preserved, and reappear when you upgrade. Zulip will never impose Slack-style time limits on your history.
Coupled with a culture of using Zulip in an organized way, it's difficult to overstate the impact it has on knowledge management... It is the essence of good UX design.
— Max McCrea, co-founder of Monadical (blog post)

Open source, without the lock-in

  • Zulip is 100% open-source software, with no "open core" catch, stewarded by the nonprofit Zulip Foundation. The paid team provides leadership, expertise and support for by far the most active open-source development community of any team chat software.
  • Zulip Cloud is a fully managed solution, operated by the team that builds Zulip. Organizations with special requirements can also self-host the same software.
  • Move your data with our high quality export and import tools; you are never locked into a proprietary tool.
