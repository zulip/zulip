See it in action

The Zulip development community is where we collaborate with hundreds of participants to improve Zulip. Many parts of the community are open for public access, so you can start exploring without creating an account.

{{ _('Check it out') }}

Going deeper

To fully experience Zulip, create an account in the development community and introduce yourself in the #new members channel.

Come back later to see how Zulip makes it easy to catch up on the conversations you’ve missed.

Please be sure to follow the community guidelines, and send any test messages to the #test here channel.

Recent conversations

You’ll see a list of recent conversations as soon as you open the app. Each one is labeled with a topic by the person who started it.

Channels

Click on the name of a channel to open a list of its recent conversation topics. For example, explore discussions of Zulip design changes in #design, or follow bug investigations in #issues.

Topics

Click on each topic in a channel to view conversations one at a time. Notice how Zulip makes it easy to have many conversations at once, without them getting in each other’s way.
