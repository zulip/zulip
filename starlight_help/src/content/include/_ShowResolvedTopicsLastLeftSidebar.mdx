By default, the [left sidebar](/help/left-sidebar) lists the topics in a
channel by recency. For channels where you mostly want to see topics that
are still open, you can instead have resolved topics listed after
unresolved ones. When you [show all topics](/help/left-sidebar#show-all-topics-in-a-channel)
in such a channel, you can click **Resolved topics** at the bottom of the
left sidebar to jump to the resolved topics. You can also still find them
by searching for a topic or
[filtering](/help/left-sidebar#filter-by-whether-topics-are-resolved) the
list to resolved topics.
