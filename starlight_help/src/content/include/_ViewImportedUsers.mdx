## View imported user accounts

You can see and manage a list of imported users who have not logged in yet to
activate their Zulip account. These users do not receive [message notification
emails](/help/email-notifications#message-notification-emails) or [digest
emails](/help/digest-emails) until they log in.

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="settings/imported" />

  1. Select the **Imported** tab.
</FlattenedSteps>
