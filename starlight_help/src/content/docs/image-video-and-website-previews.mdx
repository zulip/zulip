Zulip displays previews of images, videos, audio files and websites in your
message feed. You can [configure](#configure-how-animated-images-are-played) how
animated images are previewed,
[remove](#remove-a-link-preview-from-your-message) a link preview from your own
message, or [hide link previews](#hide-or-show-link-previews-on-a-message) on a
message just for yourself.

Organization administrators can:

* Configure the [size of image and video
  previews](#configure-image-and-video-thumbnail-size).
* Configure whether [website
  previews](#configure-whether-website-previews-are-shown) are shown.
* Configure whether [previews of linked images and
  videos](#configure-whether-previews-of-linked-images-and-videos-are-shown) are
  shown.

## Remove a link preview from your message

When a message contains a link, Zulip may show a preview of the linked content.
If a preview isn't helpful, you can remove it. Removing a preview does not remove
the link itself, and it removes the preview for everyone.

You can remove a preview from any message whose content you can
[edit](/help/edit-a-message). There is currently no way to restore a removed
preview.

Files uploaded to Zulip are part of the message rather than a preview of a link,
so they can only be removed by [editing the message](/help/edit-a-message).

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    1. Hover over the preview you want to remove.
    1. Click the **remove link preview** (<CloseIcon />) button in the upper
       right corner of the preview.
    1. Approve by clicking **Confirm**.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Configure how animated images are played

In the desktop and web apps, you can configure previews of animated images to
always show the animation, show it when you hover over the image with your
mouse, or not show it at all. For large animated images, only the first part of
the animation will be shown in the preview.

You can always see the full animated image by opening it in the [image
viewer](/help/view-images-and-videos).

<ZulipNote>
  This configuration applies only to images uploaded since July 21, 2024 on
  Zulip Cloud, or on Zulip Server [9.0+](/help/view-zulip-version) in
  self-hosted organizations. Previews of images uploaded earlier are always
  animated.
</ZulipNote>

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/preferences" />

      1. Under **Information**, select the desired option from the **Play animated
         images** dropdown.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Hide or show link previews on a message

If a message shows a [website
preview](#configure-whether-website-previews-are-shown) or a video preview from
a site like YouTube or Vimeo that you'd rather not see, you can hide it just for
yourself, without changing how the message looks for anyone else.

<FlattenedSteps>
  <MessageActionsMenu />

  1. Click **Hide link previews**.
</FlattenedSteps>

<ZulipTip>
  To bring the previews back, follow the same steps and click **Show link
  previews**.
</ZulipTip>

<KeyboardTip>
  You can use <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>L</kbd> to hide or show link previews on
  the selected message.
</KeyboardTip>

## Configure image and video thumbnail size

<AdminOnly />

You can configure the size of image and video previews for users in your
organization. Smaller previews avoid disrupting the flow of conversation, while
larger ones reduce the need to open the [image
viewer](/help/view-images-and-videos).

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="settings/organization-settings" />

  1. Under **Message feed settings**, select the desired option from the **Size
     of images and videos in messages** dropdown.

  <SaveChanges />
</FlattenedSteps>

## Configure whether website previews are shown

<AdminOnly />

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="settings/organization-settings" />

  1. Under **Message feed settings**, toggle **Show previews of linked websites**.

  <SaveChanges />
</FlattenedSteps>

## Configure whether previews of linked images and videos are shown

<AdminOnly />

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="settings/organization-settings" />

  1. Under **Message feed settings**, toggle **Show previews of linked images
     and videos**.

  <SaveChanges />
</FlattenedSteps>

## Security

To prevent images from being used to track Zulip users, Zulip proxies all
external images in messages through the server.
