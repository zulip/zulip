You can configure desktop and audible notifications for
[reactions](/help/emoji-reactions) to your messages.

## Configure notifications

These settings will affect notifications for reactions to your messages.

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="settings/notifications" />

  1. In the **Notification triggers** table, toggle the settings for **Reactions**.
</FlattenedSteps>

<ZulipTip>
  You won't be notified about reactions from [muted users](/help/mute-a-user),
  reactions in [muted topics](/help/mute-a-topic) and [muted
  channels](/help/mute-a-channel), or reactions to a message that is on
  your screen when the reaction arrives.
</ZulipTip>
