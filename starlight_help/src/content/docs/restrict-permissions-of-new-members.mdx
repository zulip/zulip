In large Zulip organizations where
[anyone can join](/help/restrict-account-creation#set-whether-invitations-are-required-to-join), it can
be useful to restrict what new members can do, to make it easier to cope
with spammers and confused users.

Members are **new members** from the moment they join until their account
ages beyond a certain **waiting period** threshold. After that, they are
**full members**. You can configure how long the waiting period is.

Zulip supports restricting access to certain features only to full members.
Such features include, but are not limited to,
[creating channels](/help/configure-who-can-create-channels),
[inviting users to the organization](/help/invite-new-users), and
[adding custom emoji](/help/custom-emoji#change-who-can-add-custom-emoji).

<ZulipTip>
  The **full members** [role](/help/user-roles) only appears as an option
  for permission settings after a waiting period for new members has
  been set.
</ZulipTip>

## Set waiting period for new members

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="settings/organization-permissions" />

  1. Under **Joining the organization**, configure
     **Waiting period before new members turn into full members**.

  <SaveChanges />
</FlattenedSteps>
