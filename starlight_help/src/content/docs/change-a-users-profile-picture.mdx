By default, users can [change their own profile
picture](/help/change-your-profile-picture), though organization administrators
can [restrict profile picture changes](/help/restrict-profile-picture-changes).

Organization administrators can always change any user's profile picture, even
when profile picture changes are restricted. This is useful for organizations
that maintain a consistent style for profile pictures, or that need to replace
a picture that is no longer appropriate.

Deleting a user's profile picture resets it to the automatically generated
picture in the style [configured](/help/configure-default-profile-pictures) by
your organization.

## Change a user's profile picture

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Via user profile">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <ManageThisUser />

      1. Click on the profile picture on the right, and choose an image to
         upload.
      1. Crop the image as desired, and click **Save changes**.
    </FlattenedSteps>

    <ManageUserTabTip />
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Via organization settings">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/users" />

      1. Find the user you would like to manage. Click the
         **manage user** (<UserCogIcon />) icon
         to the right of their name.
      1. Click on the profile picture on the right, and choose an image to
         upload.
      1. Crop the image as desired, and click **Save changes**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Delete a user's profile picture

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Via user profile">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <ManageThisUser />

      1. Hover over the profile picture on the right, and click the <CloseIcon />
         icon in the top right corner.
      1. Approve by clicking **Confirm**.
    </FlattenedSteps>

    <ManageUserTabTip />
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="Via organization settings">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/users" />

      1. Find the user you would like to manage. Click the
         **manage user** (<UserCogIcon />) icon
         to the right of their name.
      1. Hover over the profile picture on the right, and click the <CloseIcon />
         icon in the top right corner.
      1. Approve by clicking **Confirm**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
